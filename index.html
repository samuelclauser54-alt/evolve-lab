THE ARENA

Enter your AI. The best 100 race in a video.

Train a flyer above, then enter the best one from your last generation. It is scored on the arena course, where every gap is 3.5% smaller than the one before, over 5 courses it has never seen (300 gates in total). The top 100 on the board fly against each other in a reel on @atc5.builds with their nicknames on screen.

Finish at least one generation first.

    What is happening

    1. Generation 1 is random. Every network starts with random numbers, so almost all of them crash straight away.
    2. Score. Each network gets one number back: how far it got. It is never told what it did wrong.
    3. Select. The top 10% survive unchanged.
    4. Mutate. The rest of the next generation are copies of survivors with small random changes. Repeat.

    This is called neuroevolution. Things to try: set mutation to the maximum and watch learning fall apart, or shrink the population to 20 and see how much longer it takes. Every run is different because the starting numbers are random.

    What the networks see. Gates: how far the next gap is above or below, own vertical speed, distance to the gate, and the gap after that (4 inputs, 6 hidden neurons, 1 output: flap or not). Driving: 5 distances to the track edge, like laser beams (5 inputs, 6 hidden neurons, 1 output: steering). Basketball: only the distance to the hoop (1 input, 6 hidden neurons, 2 outputs: launch angle and speed); the rim height, ball size and gravity are real, and only clean makes count. Each generation gets a new random course, so they cannot memorise one.

    Made by @atc5.builds, where a new simulation is posted every day. Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you press “Enter my best AI”, which sends your nickname and that network's 37 numbers. The page also adds 1 to an anonymous counter of visits and of generations trained: no cookies, no IDs.
